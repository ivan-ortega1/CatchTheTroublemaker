★ Catch the Troublemaker

A shared-map party game · 2–6 players

One kid. Fifty coins. Two very tired parents.

Two families share one random map. Parents can only catch their own child. Troublemakers just want to fill their pockets and disappear.

●● ●● ■▲ ★ 50 ★
01

The rules

A troublemaker wins the moment they hold 50 coins. Parents win by grounding both troublemakers — or by running out the clock.

02

Family only

Dad A and Mum A can catch Troublemaker A only. Wrong-family tags do nothing. Each kid has three lives and drops every coin when caught.

03

Keeping it fair

Kids run faster; parents sprint harder and can confiscate coins. Empty seats become bots, and nobody starts next to their own parents.
